Agile - An umbrella term for all methodologies used during software development

It focuses on
 - Individuals and interactions over processes and tools
 - Working software over comprehencive documentation
 - Customer collaboration over contract negotiations
 - Responding to change over following a plan
 - Scrum is just one of the methods in Agile

Waterfall model (older method)
 - One by one execution
 - Analysis, Requirements, Design, Implementation, Testing, Maintenance

Scrum Theory
 - Scrum is a framework under Agile for managing a cross-functional team by inspecting, adapting, improving and delivering
 - History repeats itself unless you do something about it
 - Principles - Transparency, Inspection, Adaption
 - Done means done unless development team feels it is done

Scrum Skeleton
 - Project  backlog
 - Sprint backlog is a subset of project backlog
 - Sprint backlog is broken into tasks
 - Assigned to individual teams
 - Sprint circle is 1 calendar month
 - After each sprint circle a potentially reasonable product increment is shipped

Scrum Guide
 - Written by Jeff Sutherland and Ken Schwaber
 - Scrum can be an overkill for simple projects
